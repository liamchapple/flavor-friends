The idea

A music video where the pizza is the main stage.

The Flavor Friends come together like a K-pop dance group to highlight each ingredient on the Korean BBQ Chicken Pizza: bold spicy kimchi, savory Korean-seasoned crispy chicken, smokey gochugaru and spicy gochujang. Two dance breaks keep all four in perfect sync, each hero gets a solo in their own world, the crew levels up for the crescendo, and it all lands back on one perfect slice.

Every member keeps their own look, sound and move, but they read as one family: one palette, one stage, one choreography.

How we make it

Built like a comeback.

Sound

One K-pop track that shifts with each world: a hip-hop bar for Kko-Kko, a half-time gayageum bend through Garu's flip, the full chorus for dance break 2. Each hero has a signature sound on their point.

Choreography

A Korean choreographer builds one learnable routine: two eight-counts all four dance in perfect sync, at 0:05 and at the chorus, with the solos in between.

Shoot

Real kitchen, real food, practical camera moves with the crew comped in. Busby Berkeley overheads, sweeping orbits and macro passes around the pie.

Authenticity

Korean illustrators and studios on character design, Korean names and copy reviewed by a cultural consultant, and Easter eggs for fans who know.
